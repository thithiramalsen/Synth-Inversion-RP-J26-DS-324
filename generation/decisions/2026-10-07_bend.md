# Architecture B selected — 7 October 2026

The researcher selected the one-oscillator Bend architecture following the A/B
comparison and reported: "i feel like bend introduces more variation to tone".
This is an informal design/listening judgment. It is not a participant-study
result or evidence that every part of the descriptor domain is covered.

## Selected configuration

New profile: `restricted_bend_v3`, in `generation/bend_config.py`.
The twelve variable controls, in manifest order, are:

1. Wavetable position.
2. Oscillator 1 Bend amount, with Bend mode fixed.
3. Filter cutoff.
4. Filter resonance.
5. Filter drive.
6. Amp attack.
7. Amp decay.
8. Amp sustain.
9. Amp release.
10. Filter-envelope amount.
11. Filter-envelope attack.
12. Filter-envelope decay.

Filter-envelope sustain is fixed at raw 0. Warp phase is fixed at raw .5; warp
spread is 0; spectral morph is disabled. Oscillator 2/3 and noise remain off.
Bend .5 is neutral. The provisional Bend interval is .25-.75, matching the main
comparison; 0 and 1 were stress probes, not adopted bounds. This interval is an
engineering starting point, not demonstrated DC-free or perceptually optimal.
Other ranges and rendering conditions remain those of architecture A.

## Evidence retained and next decision

[Measured A/B results](../characterization/architecture_ab_v1/REPORT.md) show
numerical Bend activity in 108/108 main comparisons and exact fresh-instance and
preset round-trip repeatability at the tested settings. Main B renders had 61/108
DC flags versus 19/108 for A, with no silence/clipping. One neutral-versus-bypass
comparison slightly exceeded its numerical tolerance; the original flag remains.
The finite-grid match screen does not establish perceptual independence.

Architecture is selected; **audio preprocessing/playback policy and final bounds
remain unresolved**. No DC correction or loudness normalization was silently
introduced. Address those decisions consistently before generating/selecting the
formal study pool. The instruction not to generate the new 1,024-sound dataset
remains in force.

The old eight-control `pilot_v1` and twelve-control `restricted_v2` profile/preset
and comparison audio are retained. A/B experiments continue to use their original
architecture-A reference. The generator and preset-builder CLI defaults select
the new version; existing model/survey datasets remain unchanged.
