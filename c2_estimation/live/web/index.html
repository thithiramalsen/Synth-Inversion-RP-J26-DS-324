METHOD

How it works

  1. Preprocess. Apply the DC filter once, extract a 64-band log-mel spectrogram and scale it using training statistics.
  2. Predict. Three CNNs estimate the controls. Their average gives the patch; their predicted variances and disagreement give uncertainty U.
  3. Render. Vital plays the patch. R measures its distance from the target. Changing one control at a time measures local sensitivity S.
  4. Score reliability. A saved calibrator maps U, S and R to q. It does not adjust the patch.
  5. Check the patch. Reload it through Vita and verify the controls and rendered audio.

Different settings can produce similar sounds. A close match does not prove the original controls were recovered.

Files stay in the local results folder. Completed runs can be reopened after a restart. No study responses are collected here.
