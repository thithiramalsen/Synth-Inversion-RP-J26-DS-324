THE C2 METHOD

What happens to your clip

  1. Prepare the sound. Apply the fixed DC rule once, extract the 64-band log-mel spectrogram and use the normalization learned from training data.
  2. Estimate the settings. Three trained CNNs predict the controls. Their average gives the patch; their variances and disagreement give uncertainty U.
  3. Render and inspect. Vital renders the predicted patch. R measures its difference from the target. Small, separate changes to each control measure local sensitivity S.
  4. Estimate reliability. The frozen development calibrator combines U, S and R to estimate correctness within the declared tolerance. It does not adjust the patch.
  5. Verify the export. Reload the saved preset through Vita and check its controls and rendered audio against the delivered prediction.

Similar sounds can come from different settings. A useful reconstruction does not prove that every original control has been recovered.

Uploads and outputs stay in this app’s local results folder. Completed runs can be reopened after a server restart. This app does not collect study responses.
