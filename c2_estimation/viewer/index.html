DEVELOPMENT EVALUATION

Validation results

Final test still pending

All methods were compared on the same 512 reliability-validation clips. The reliability models were fitted on 2,048 separate calibration clips. Validation was also used to choose regularization, so these scores still need confirmation on the test set.

On 4,096 inverse-validation clips, the ensemble's MAE was 0.127991, compared with 0.126112 for the single CNN. The ensemble did not improve parameter accuracy in this run. The comparisons below examine its reliability estimates.

C2 METHOD

How these results were produced

  1. 01

    Convert the clip to a spectrogram

    The input is a 64-band log-mel spectrogram. Its normalization uses statistics from the training set.

  2. 02

    Predict the 13 settings

    Three independently trained CNNs each predict parameter means and variances. Their combined predictions give the settings and ensemble uncertainty U.

  3. 03

    Render the predicted sound

    Vita applies the settings to the fixed Vital preset and renders a clip. Comparing this with the target gives the reconstruction error R.

  4. 04

    Change one parameter at a time

    Small lower and higher settings show how much each parameter affects the sound. This gives sensitivity S. The step size uses values read back from the synth, and the original prediction stays fixed.

  5. 05

    Estimate parameter reliability

    A logistic model for each parameter uses U, S and R to estimate the chance that the predicted value is within ±0.05 of its research range.

Current scope

The models cover one restricted Vital preset family. Performance on other sounds and synths has not been evaluated. Final test evaluation and a small human study are still pending.
